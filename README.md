# Plumo – site

Help, contact and privacy policy for Plumo – Photo Cleaner (iPhone).

- `index.html`: the whole site, in English, French and Spanish (`?lang=en|fr|es`).
- `#support`: help and contact. `#privacy`: privacy policy (linked from the app and App Store Connect).
- `assets/`: the app icon at a few sizes.

No build step, no cookies, no analytics, no external fonts or scripts.
